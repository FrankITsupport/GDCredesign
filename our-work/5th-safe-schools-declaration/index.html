5th Safe Schools Declaration Conference 2025

Date: 25–26 November 2025

Venue: Safari Park Hotel

Advancing Global Commitments to Safe Education

The 5th Safe Schools Declaration Conference brought together governments, international organizations, education leaders, and humanitarian partners to strengthen global commitments toward protecting schools, students, and educators from the effects of conflict.

Hosted in Nairobi, the conference provided a platform for dialogue, knowledge exchange, and renewed international action to safeguard access to education in fragile and conflict-affected environments.

GDC was engaged to deliver the conference environment and event execution, ensuring a professional setting capable of hosting high-level international dialogue and multi-stakeholder engagement.
