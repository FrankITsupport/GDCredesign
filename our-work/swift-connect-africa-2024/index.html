Swift Connect Africa 2024 — Powering Africa's Financial Future

Swift Connect Africa 2024, hosted by SWIFT, brought together Africa's banking and financial technology ecosystem to address the future of cross-border payments, financial security, and digital transformation.

GDC delivered full conference management and production support, ensuring a seamless, high-level experience for regional and international delegates.

Impact

The conference strengthened regional dialogue on financial connectivity, promoted secure payment innovation, and reinforced Africa's role in shaping the future of global financial systems.
