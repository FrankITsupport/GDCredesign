01 / ABOUT US

About GDC
We bring ideas to life and help them reach the people who matter.

Global Digital Centre (GDC) is a Kenya-based creative and strategic partner. We bring together six areas of expertise: Events & Experiences, PR & Communications, Branding & Digital, Interpretation & Languages, Photography & Film, and Consultancy & Research.

We create conferences, exhibitions, galas, dinners and private events that are carefully planned and confidently delivered. We shape the story around each experience through communications, branding and digital content. Our language, photography and film teams help that story travel across audiences and beyond the day itself. We begin by listening. What are you trying to achieve? Who needs to be in the room, hear the message or take action? Research and strategic thinking help us answer those questions before we build the right team and approach. From the first conversation to the final delivery, we connect the creative, technical and practical details around one goal: work that makes an impact for you and the people you want to reach.
