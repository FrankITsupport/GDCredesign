01 / ABOUT US

One partner, many ways to move an idea forward.

Global Digital Centre is a full-service Events, PR & Communications and Consultancy firm in Nairobi. We bring strategic thinking, creative design and technical expertise together for public institutions, development partners, corporates and communities.

We shape experiences, tell stories and build the systems that help organisations connect with the people who matter.
