01 / ABOUT US

Global Digital Centre (GDC) is a Kenya-based creative and strategic partner. We bring together six areas of expertise: Events & Experiences, PR & Communications, Branding & Digital, Interpretation & Languages, Photography & Film, and Consultancy & Research.

We begin by listening. What are you trying to achieve? Who needs to be in the room, hear the message or take action? Research and strategic thinking help us answer those questions before we build the right team and approach. From the first conversation to the final delivery, we connect the creative, technical and practical details around one goal: work that makes an impact for you and the people you want to reach.

03 / HOW WE WORK

One goal. The right people.
Every detail connected.

Great work starts with understanding what success means to you. Whether you need an event, a campaign, a film or strategic advice, we build our approach around your audience and the result you want to achieve.

Your brief. One connected team.
  1. 01

    We listen and learn

    We start with your brief, your audience and your goals. We ask the questions that help us understand what matters most, what needs to happen and how we will measure success.

    THE OUTCOMEA shared direction
  2. 02

    We shape the approach

    We develop a clear idea and a practical plan. Depending on the project, we bring together the right expertise across events, communications, branding, digital, languages, film and research.

    THE OUTCOMEA clear plan
  3. 03

    We bring it to life

    Our teams manage the creative work, production and delivery together. You have clear points of contact, agreed milestones and visibility as the work progresses.

    THE OUTCOMEYour vision, made real
  4. 04

    We learn from the results

    After delivery, we look at what reached people, how they responded and what could work harder next time. We share those insights with you so the value of the work continues beyond the project.

    THE OUTCOMEInsight for your next move

In conversation at every stage. Each project makes the next one better.
