# Services imagery

All six service cards use a distinct photo from the existing GDC project galleries. Each WebP is 900×506; together they total about 371 KB.

| Service | Asset | Original project image |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Events & experiences | `events.webp` | `assets/media/projects/swift/gallery-2.jpg` — conference venue |
| PR & communications | `communications.webp` | `assets/media/projects/nssf/gallery-3.jpg` — branded conference stage and messaging |
| Branding & digital | `branding.webp` | `assets/media/projects/swift/gallery-4.webp` — branded exhibition environment |
| Interpretation & languages | `interpretation.webp` | `assets/media/projects/issa/gallery-4.jpg` — interpretation booths |
| Photography & film | `film.webp` | `assets/media/projects/iea/gallery-3.jpg` — conference filming equipment |
| Consultancy & research | `consultancy.webp` | `assets/media/projects/apra/gallery-4.jpg` — investment forum meeting environment |

These are decorative service illustrations, with matching dark gradients for legible text. The original files remain in their case-study galleries. Change a service's `image` field in `content/site.json` to replace its photo.
